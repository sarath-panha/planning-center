Section 7.2: Comprehensive Diagnostic Suite
* Headline (H2): Advanced Capture Technologies
* 3D Radiography (CBCT & CT): High-resolution Cone Beam Computed Tomography with adjustable fields of view (FOV). Essential for implant planning, endodontics, and oral surgery mapping.
* 2D Radiography (Pano & Ceph): Crystal-clear Panoramic x-rays for general diagnostics and Cephalometric imaging specifically calibrated for precise orthodontic tracing and planning.

* 3D Facial Scanning: Capturing the patient's soft tissue in motion and rest. Crucial for aligning the dental arch to facial midlines in complex full-arch and aesthetic rehabilitations.
* Clinical Photography & Video: Professional DSLR studio setups capturing exact macro shade references, retracter shots, and dynamic smile videos for Digital Smile Design (DSD).

* Seamless Workflow Integration: If we are handling your CAD design or implant planning, capturing the data at our center eliminates transfer times and data compatibility errors.
* Patient Comfort: A luxurious, stress-free environment operated by highly trained, compassionate radiological technicians.
* Calibration & Maintenance: Our hardware is meticulously calibrated daily to ensure absolute scale accuracy—critical for surgical guides and prosthetics.
Section 7.4: What You Receive
* Instant digital access to patient files via our secure, HIPAA-compliant online portal.
* Exported DICOM files (for CBCT/CT), high-res JPEGs/PNGs (for photos), and open STL/PLY/OBJ files (for surface and face scans).
* Dedicated 3D viewing software links, allowing you to manipulate and view patient scans without needing expensive local software.
